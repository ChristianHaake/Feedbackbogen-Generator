# Usage notes

This English translation was generated by AI. Only the German version is legally binding.

You can use the generator to create assessment sheets free of charge without an account. Financial support is voluntary.

## Choosing criteria and adding your own content

Check that the suggested criteria suit your lesson and your students. If you include someone else’s text, make sure you can use it for your intended purpose and share it if you plan to distribute the sheet.

## Backing up your work

Your sheet is saved locally in your browser. Export important sheets as JSON project files so you can reopen and edit them later, even after clearing the site data.
